Year2026

Case notes

Challenge, solution and outcome.

Challenge

Most barber websites are either too plain or too generic. Cut Culture needed to feel local, loud and commercial at the same time.

Solution

The design uses oversized condensed typography, black backgrounds, punchy colour blocks and clear service pricing to create immediate personality.

Outcome

A strong local-service concept that shows how a small business can feel confident, memorable and easy to contact online.

Build notes

Original concept build.

This project was created as original VraxisWeb portfolio work and built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript. The layout, art direction and interface structure were designed to show how a real business in this category could present itself online.
