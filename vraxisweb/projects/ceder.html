Case notes

Challenge, solution and outcome.

Challenge

The project needed to make a restaurant feel established while still being simple enough for customers to understand quickly.

Solution

The site combines strong serif typography, dark atmosphere, food galleries, clear prices and a direct WhatsApp reservation route.

Outcome

A sharp restaurant concept that works as proof for hospitality businesses needing menu, location and booking clarity.

Build notes

Original concept build.

This project was created as original VraxisWeb portfolio work and built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript. The layout, art direction and interface structure were designed to show how a real business in this category could present itself online.
