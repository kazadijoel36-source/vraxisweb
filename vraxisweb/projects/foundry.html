Foundry Coffee

A warm coffee shop concept focused on calm typography, menu clarity and everyday lifestyle storytelling.

Year2026
TypeFood & beverage

Case notes

Challenge, solution and outcome.

Challenge

This project needed to move away from dark luxury and prove the studio could create something softer, brighter and more approachable.

Solution

Foundry uses a light editorial layout, warm copy, coffee imagery and clear menu sections to create a friendly local café experience.

Outcome

A complete café concept with homepage, menu, story and image-led sections — relevant for coffee shops, bakeries and lifestyle hospitality brands.

Build notes

Original concept build.

This project was created as original VraxisWeb portfolio work and built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript. The layout, art direction and interface structure were designed to show how a real business in this category could present itself online.
